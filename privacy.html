OpenClaw privacy policy

OpenClaw is a personal tool used only by its owner, Robert Assaf.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission, OpenClaw reads the owner's own Google account data (Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Google Docs and Contacts) to help him manage his own email, calendar and household tasks.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is processed on the owner's own computer and stored there or in the owner's own Google account.
  • Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and is not used for advertising.
  • No data about anyone other than the owner's own account is collected through Google sign-in.

Google API Services

OpenClaw's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove OpenClaw's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions can be sent to the support email address shown on the Google sign-in screen for OpenClaw.
